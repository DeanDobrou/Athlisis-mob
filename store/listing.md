## Reviewer notes (Apple: App Review Information → Notes; Google: App access)

Demo account: `[demo email]` / `[demo password]`. Please don't change its email or password.

Athlisis is the members' app for a single gym in Greece. There is no sign-up in the app: the gym creates each member's account when they join.

The demo account has an active membership and a few booked classes. To try it: the Πρόγραμμα tab lists the week's classes; tap one to book it (Κλείσε θέση) or cancel a booking (Ακύρωση κράτησης). Cancelling closes 60 minutes before a class starts, and a member can book one class per day. The Λογαριασμός tab has the membership, profile, email and password settings, the privacy policy, and account deletion.

Payments: memberships are paid in person at the gym. The app sells nothing and has no in-app purchases.

Account deletion: Λογαριασμός → Διαγραφή λογαριασμού opens https://as-athlisis.gr/privacy#delete, which explains how to ask for it.
